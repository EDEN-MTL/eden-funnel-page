The system

Six components. One system.

Everything a brokerage needs to turn a lead into a closed deal — built as one connected system, not six separate tools.

01

Unified Intake

Every lead source — Zillow, Meta, your website, referrals — flows into one place instead of five disconnected inboxes.

02

AI ISA

An AI-run inside sales agent qualifies every lead the moment it arrives — through dedicated qualification forms and live AI-led conversations, day or night. Your team never calls a raw, unscored contact — every lead reaching them has already been vetted.

03

Route & Book

Qualified leads are routed to the right agent and booked directly onto the calendar — no manual handoff required.

04

Nurture & Reactivation

Leads that aren't ready yet get worked on a long-term cadence instead of disappearing into a dead CRM.

05

Content OS

Content and campaigns are built with a direct line back to the pipeline, not run as a disconnected side project.

06

Owner Dashboard

One dashboard shows exactly where every lead stands and what's actually producing — no more guessing which vendor is working.
