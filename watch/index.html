Common questions

Before you get on the call

What if it doesn't work for my market?+
That's exactly why we only take 4 teams a month — one per market. We're not mass-producing this. It's built around your specific listings, your area, and your agents' availability.
I've tried AI chatbots before and they were useless.+
This isn't a chatbot on your website hoping someone types a question. This is outbound — it calls people, texts people, follows up like a real ISA would, except it never sleeps and never quits.
How do I know the leads will actually be good?+
Every lead gets graded before your agents see it. You're not getting a pile of names — you're getting a short list of pre-qualified, graded appointments.
What if my agents don't trust leads from an AI system?+
Every appointment comes with a full brief — who the person is, their budget, their timeline, what they're looking for. Your agents walk in more prepared than they would with a typical lead handoff from a human ISA.
This sounds expensive.+
Compare it to what you're already spending. Zillow Flex takes 35-40% of your commission per closing — on a single $400K deal that's around $5,000 gone. An agency retainer alone is $24,000 a year, and you still don't own anything. When we go through your numbers on the call, you'll see it stacks up well against what you're already paying for something that isn't even working as hard for you.
What if I sign up and it just doesn't perform?+
There's no long-term contract. And remember — we're the ones funding your first $2,000 in ads. We have as much reason to make this work as you do.
How much time is this going to take from me or my agents?+
Almost none. We build it, we run it, we optimize it. Your agents' only job is showing up to appointments that are already booked and qualified.
Why should I trust a company I've never heard of over an established platform like Zillow?+
We've worked with over 100 agents and teams across real estate, so this isn't our first run at solving this problem. And Zillow's business model depends on you staying dependent on them — ours depends on the system actually working, because we only get paid monthly if you keep us around, and you can walk away anytime since you own everything. It's already working on this specific system too — one team's agents are averaging an extra deal a month each since going live, and another added $30,000 in GCI in their first four months.
What happens after the 4 September spots are gone?+
We reopen in October, but the pricing and the funded ad spend offer may not be the same — this specific offer is tied to the September batch.
